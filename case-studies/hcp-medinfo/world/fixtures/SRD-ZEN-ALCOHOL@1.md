## Question

Can patients drink alcohol while taking Zentrava?

## Summary

No interaction between Zentrava and alcohol was seen in a dedicated study. Patients who drink should keep to moderate amounts.

## Data

In study ZEN-DDI-03, 18 healthy adults took Zentrava 20 mg with and without 0.5 g/kg of alcohol. Zentrava exposure did not change, and alcohol levels did not change.
