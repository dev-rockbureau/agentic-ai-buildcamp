## Question

Can Zentrava tablets be crushed or split?

## Summary

Zentrava tablets may be crushed and mixed with about 30 mL of water, then taken right away. Crushed tablets gave the same blood levels as whole tablets.

## Data

In study ZEN-BE-07, 24 healthy adults took one 20 mg tablet either whole or crushed in water. Exposure was the same within 5% for both ways of taking it.

## Not studied

Giving crushed tablets through a feeding tube has not been studied.

## References
