## Indication

Zentrava (synthetic drug, zentravimod) 20 mg film-coated tablets treat moderate to severe chronic inflammatory arthritis in adults.

## Dosage

The recommended dose is 20 mg once daily. The maximum daily dose is 20 mg.

## Renal impairment

For patients with a creatinine clearance of 30 to 59 mL/min, the dose is 10 mg once daily. Zentrava is not recommended below 30 mL/min.

## Administration

Swallow the tablet whole with a glass of water. Zentrava can be taken with or without food.

## Contraindications

Hypersensitivity to zentravimod or to any of the excipients.

## Special warnings: skin reactions

Severe skin reactions, including rash with blistering, have been reported. Treatment must be stopped if a severe skin reaction occurs.

## Undesirable effects

Very common or common undesirable effects were headache (8%), nausea (5%) and rash (3%).

## Paediatric population

The safety and efficacy of Zentrava in children and adolescents under 18 years have not been established. No data are available.

## Pregnancy

There are no data on the use of Zentrava in pregnant women. Zentrava is not recommended during pregnancy.

## Storage

Do not store above 25 °C. Store in the original package to protect from moisture.
