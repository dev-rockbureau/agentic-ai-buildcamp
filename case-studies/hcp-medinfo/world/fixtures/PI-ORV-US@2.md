## Indication

Orvelan (synthetic drug, orvelastat) 5 mg tablets lower high blood pressure in adults.

## Dosage

The recommended dose is 5 mg once daily. The dose may be increased to 10 mg once daily after 4 weeks. The maximum daily dose is 10 mg.

## Missed dose

If a dose is missed, take it as soon as possible on the same day. Do not take two doses on the same day.

## Drug interactions

Grapefruit juice raises orvelastat levels. Patients should avoid large amounts of grapefruit juice.

## Adverse reactions

The most common adverse reactions were dizziness (6%) and swelling of the ankles (4%).

## Storage

Store at 20 to 25 °C.
