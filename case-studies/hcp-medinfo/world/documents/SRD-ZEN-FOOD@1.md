## Question

Should Zentrava be taken with food?

## Summary

Zentrava can be taken with or without food. A high-fat meal raised Zentrava exposure by 15%, which is not expected to matter clinically.

## Data

In study ZEN-FE-02, 20 healthy adults took Zentrava 20 mg fasting and after a high-fat meal. Exposure was 15% higher after the meal.
