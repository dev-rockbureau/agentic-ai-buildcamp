## Indication

Orvelan (synthetic drug, orvelastat) 5 mg tablets treat essential hypertension in adults.

## Dosage

The usual dose is 5 mg once daily. If needed, the dose can be raised to 10 mg once daily after 4 weeks. Do not exceed 10 mg per day.

## Missed dose

A missed dose should be taken as soon as remembered on the same day. A double dose must not be taken to make up for a missed one.

## Interactions

Grapefruit juice increases exposure to orvelastat. Large amounts of grapefruit juice should be avoided.

## Undesirable effects

Common undesirable effects were dizziness (6%) and ankle oedema (4%).

## Storage

Do not store above 25 °C.
