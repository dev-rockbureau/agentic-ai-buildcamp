## Question

Can Zentrava be used in children?

## Summary

Zentrava is not approved for patients under 18 years. This response shares unpublished study data, because you asked for it.

## Data

In the open-label study ZEN-PED-01, 12 children aged 8 to 17 took Zentrava 10 mg once daily for 12 weeks. Headache was reported in 2 of 12 children. No serious adverse events were reported.

## Not studied

No data are available for children under 8 years.
