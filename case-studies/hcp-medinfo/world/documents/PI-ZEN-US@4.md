## Indication

Zentrava (synthetic drug, zentravimod) 20 mg tablets treat moderate to severe chronic inflammatory arthritis in adults.

## Dosage

The recommended dose is 20 mg taken by mouth once daily. The maximum daily dose is 20 mg.

## Renal impairment

For patients with an eGFR of 30 to 59 mL/min, the dose is 10 mg once daily. Zentrava is not recommended when the eGFR is below 30 mL/min.

## Administration

Swallow the tablet with water, with or without food. Take it at the same time each day.

## Contraindications

Do not use Zentrava in patients with a known allergy to zentravimod or any of its ingredients.

## Warnings: serious skin reactions

Serious skin reactions, including severe rash, have been reported. Stop Zentrava if a severe rash or blistering appears, and tell the patient to seek medical care.

## Adverse reactions

The most common adverse reactions in clinical trials were headache (8%), nausea (5%) and rash (3%).

## Use in children

The safety and effectiveness of Zentrava in patients under 18 years have not been established.

## Pregnancy

There are no adequate data on Zentrava in pregnant women. Use only if the benefit justifies the risk to the fetus.

## Storage

Store at 20 to 25 °C. Keep the tablets in the original bottle to protect them from moisture.
