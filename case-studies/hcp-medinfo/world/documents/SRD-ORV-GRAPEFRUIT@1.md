## Question

Can patients taking Orvelan drink grapefruit juice?

## Summary

Patients should avoid large amounts of grapefruit juice. One glass of 250 mL a day raised orvelastat exposure by 40%.

## Data

In study ORV-DDI-05, 16 healthy adults took Orvelan 5 mg with 250 mL of grapefruit juice daily for 7 days. Orvelastat exposure rose by 40%.
